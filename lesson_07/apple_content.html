Apple

An apple tree is a small to medium-sized deciduous tree known for its edible fruit, the apple. These trees typically grow to around 30 feet tall and wide. They have oval-shaped, dark green leaves with a serrated edge, and in the spring, they produce white to pale pink blossoms. The fruit, which matures in the fall, can be green, yellow, or red, and is typically about 2-4 inches in diameter.
