Maple

Maples are trees and shrubs belonging to the genus Acer. There are over 100 species of maple trees, and they are found throughout Asia, Europe, North Africa, and North America. Maple trees are best known for their unique leaf shapes and bright fall colors.

Maple trees grow in many different environments, including forests, parks, and gardens. They are valued for producing maple syrup, their strong wood used for furniture and flooring, and their use as decorative landscape trees.
